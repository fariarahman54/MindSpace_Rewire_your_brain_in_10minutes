# MindSpace: Rewire your brain in 10 minutes 

A free, six-week course on metacognition and neuroplasticity that takes about ten minutes a day.

I wanted to understand how learning actually works: how to think about my own thinking, and how the brain physically changes when we practise something. Most of what I found was either long books or dense research papers, and I'm busy. So I read through the research, turned it into one short idea a day, and built a tracker I could tick off every day. Each day teaches me something new and gives me something small to try.

**Try it here:** 
(https://fariarahman54.github.io/MindSpace_Rewire_your_brain_in_10minutes/)

## What a day looks like

Every day has four small things to tick:

- **Read**: a one-paragraph idea about metacognition or neuroplasticity, written in plain language, with its source
- **Two small tasks**: something to try in real life or at work, plus a reflection, recall or practice task
- **Quiz**: one multiple-choice question on the day's idea. It's ticked once you get it right, with a short explanation of why

Each completed day lights up on the map at the top, and consecutive days link together. There's also space for a one-line note each day.

## The six weeks

1. **Noticing your own thinking**: what metacognition is, and the plan, monitor, evaluate loop
2. **Study methods that actually work**: retrieval practice, spacing, interleaving, elaboration
3. **How the brain changes**: synaptic plasticity, myelin, the "double-edged sword" of plasticity, and brain myths
4. **Building skills deliberately**: deliberate practice, feedback, chunking, learning from mistakes
5. **A stronger brain**: sleep, exercise, stress and focus
6. **Your personal learning system**: turning it all into habits that last

From Week 3 onwards there's also a two-minute "tiny skill" to practise daily, so you can watch your own brain change over the course.

## Your progress stays private

There's no sign-up and no server. Your ticks and notes are saved only in your own browser on your own device. That also means progress doesn't sync between devices, and clearing your browser data will reset it.

## Sources

The daily readings are my own short summaries. These two open-access reviews are the main sources, and both are free to read:

- Fleur, D. S., Bredeweg, B., & van den Bos, W. (2021). Metacognition: ideas and insights from neuro- and educational sciences. *npj Science of Learning*, 6, 13. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00089-5
- Gazerani, P. (2025). The neuroplastic brain: current breakthroughs and emerging frontiers. *Brain Research*, 1858, 149643. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brainres.2025.149643

Other ideas come from:

- Roediger, H. L., & Karpicke, J. D. (2006). Test-enhanced learning. *Psychological Science*, 17(3), 249–255.
- Erickson, K. I., et al. (2011). Exercise training increases size of hippocampus and improves memory. *PNAS*, 108(7), 3017–3022.
- Anders Ericsson's research on deliberate practice, described in *Peak* (Ericsson & Pool, 2016)
- Barbara Oakley's *A Mind for Numbers* and her free Coursera course *Learning How to Learn*

## How it's built

It's a single `index.html` file with no frameworks and no build step, hosted with GitHub Pages. To change or add content, edit the course data near the bottom of the file.
